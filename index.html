04 / INDIA IN SPACE

India's journey
to the stars.

India's space programme uses science and technology to explore space and support life on Earth.

🚀

ISRO • INDIA

SPACE EXPLORATION

Curiosity with a purpose.

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has developed important capabilities in satellite communication, navigation, weather services and scientific research.

Visit Official ISRO Website ↗
2008

Chandrayaan-1

India's first lunar mission helped strengthen evidence for water molecules on the Moon.

2014

Mangalyaan

India's Mars Orbiter Mission reached Mars orbit in its first attempt.

2023

Chandrayaan-3

India achieved a successful soft landing near the Moon's south polar region.

2023

Aditya-L1

India's first space-based solar observatory mission studies the Sun.
