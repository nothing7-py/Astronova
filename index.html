Explore the Planets

Mercury

The smallest planet and closest to the Sun. Its heavily cratered surface is covered with ancient impact marks.

  • Year: 88 Earth days
  • Moons: None

Venus

A rocky world wrapped in thick clouds. Its powerful greenhouse effect makes it hotter than Mercury.

  • Year: 225 Earth days
  • Moons: None

Earth

Our home is the only world we know to support life, with liquid water on its surface and a protective atmosphere.

  • Year: 365 days
  • Moons: 1

Mars

Iron-rich dust gives Mars its red color. Scientists study its rocks and landscape for clues about ancient water.

  • Year: 687 Earth days
  • Moons: 2

Jupiter

The largest planet, with swirling cloud bands and the Great Red Spot, a giant storm observed for centuries.

  • Year: About 12 Earth years
  • Moons: Many; the count changes as new ones are confirmed

Saturn

Its bright rings are made mostly of countless pieces of ice and rock, from tiny grains to much larger chunks.

  • Year: About 29 Earth years
  • Moons: Many; the count changes as new ones are confirmed

Uranus

This pale-blue ice giant spins almost on its side, giving it unusually extreme seasons.

  • Year: 84 Earth years
  • Moons: 28 known

Neptune

The most distant planet from the Sun has a deep-blue atmosphere and some of the fastest winds measured in the solar system.

  • Year: About 165 Earth years
  • Moons: 16 known

Cosmic Mystery Corner

🌀 Black Holes

Regions of space where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape from inside the event horizon. Black holes can form when very massive stars collapse.

☁ Nebulae

Huge clouds of gas and dust. Gravity can pull parts of a nebula together until new stars form; some nebulae are also the remains of dying stars.

🌌 Galaxies

Massive systems of stars, gas, dust and dark matter held together by gravity. Our Sun belongs to the Milky Way galaxy.

India's Journey in Space

1975

Aryabhata

India's first satellite was launched to study X-ray astronomy, solar physics and the Earth's upper atmosphere.

1980

Rohini

Rohini RS-1 became the first satellite launched into orbit by an Indian-made launch vehicle, SLV-3.

2008

Chandrayaan-1

India's first lunar mission mapped the Moon and helped find evidence of water molecules on its surface.

2013

Mangalyaan

The Mars Orbiter Mission made India the first country to reach Mars orbit on its first attempt.

2023

Chandrayaan-3

Its Vikram lander and Pragyan rover explored the Moon's south polar region after a successful soft landing.

2023

Aditya-L1

India's first space-based solar observatory studies the Sun from a special vantage point called the Sun-Earth L1 region.
